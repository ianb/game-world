# Farm animals

`chicken_*.png`, `sheep_*.png`, `cow_*.png` and `pig_*.png` are from
[LPC style farm animals](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-style-farm-animals)
(https://opengameart.org/node/11629) by Daniel Eddeland (daneeklu), licensed
CC-BY 3.0 or later, or GNU GPL 2.0 or later. Attribution as the author asks:
Daniel Eddeland, OpenGameArt.org.

Each walk sheet has four rows (facing up, left, down, right) of four frames.
Chickens are 32 px a frame; the others 128 px.
