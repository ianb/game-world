# Elias

Lives in the west end, the vicarage. Program: `crates/characters/src/elias.rs`.

## Description

The priest. Kindly, lonely, discreet. Takes confidences and even complaints as trust.

Placeholder, written to get the valley running. Rename and rewrite at will.

## Design notes

- On weekday afternoons he makes a round: of those resting or at home within 40 tiles, he calls on whoever he knows least (lowest familiarity).
- Leads the Sunday service; Agnes, Bess, Jenna, Kit, Hester, Lottie, Martha and Celia attend.
- Accepts Celia's tea invitations like Agnes.

Ideas for later:

- Could prefer people he has heard are in trouble, using the `trouble` marks on topics.
