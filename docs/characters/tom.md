# Tom

Lives in the old town, the tavern; sleeps upstairs in the house. Program: `worlds/valley/characters/src/tom.rs`.

## Description

Keeps the tavern. Blunt, teases his regulars, carries a mood and a grievance through the day. Sundays off fishing at the pond.

Placeholder, written to get the valley running. Rename and rewrite at will.

## Design notes

- Weeds the tavern yard (`tavern-yard` area) before opening when it is overgrown.
- Grievance builds from trips, a breadless bar, empty evenings and overheard complaints; past a point the next person he talks to gets a dig.
- Now has many more regulars (Rob, Ned, Jory, Dan, Moss). Ale comes from Hal; buys a barrel from Rob on Fridays and firewood from Abel on Saturdays.
