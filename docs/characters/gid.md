# Gid

Lives in the river, the mill. Program: `crates/characters/src/gid.rs`.

## Description

The miller. Superstitious.

Placeholder, written to get the valley running. Rename and rewrite at will.

## Design notes

- Grinds flour at the millstone; takes flour to the bakery on Thursdays.
- Does not mill on Fridays (schedule) and is guarded with everyone that day.
- A fall means the day is against him: he goes home and stays (`given_up`).
