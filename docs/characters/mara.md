# Mara

Lives in the old town, the bakery; sleeps upstairs in the house. Program: `worlds/valley/characters/src/mara.rs`.

## Description

The baker. Friendly and discreet at the counter. Fond of Tom. Keeps Hal at a distance once she knows he is sweet on her.

Placeholder, written to get the valley running. Rename and rewrite at will.

## Design notes

- Bakes when the shelf is bare and there is flour in the pantry; flour now comes from Hal and, on Thursdays, from Gid at the mill.
- Takes Tom a pastry when he seemed low the last time they talked.
- Evenings at the tavern she goes back to the bar for another word with Tom, and compliments him.
- Buys honey from Martha on Fridays and flour from Gid on Thursdays; both are paid from her coins, so watch her money in `economy`.

- With 45 villagers the bakery is the only bread for most of the valley; a second oven in the hamlet would take pressure off.
