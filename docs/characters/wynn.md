# Wynn

Lives in south of the old town, the herbalist's cottage. Program: `crates/characters/src/wynn.rs`.

## Description

The herbalist. Quiet, practical, kind.

Placeholder, written to get the valley running. Rename and rewrite at will.

## Design notes

- On Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings she gathers along the `wood-edge` area, adding remedies to her shelf.
- When she meets someone she saw or heard come to grief in the last day, she offers a remedy; they pay if they can.
- Rarely sells: few people she meets have had a fall. That is acceptable for now.

Ideas for later:

- Notice need as well as mishaps (someone urgently thirsty or hungry).
