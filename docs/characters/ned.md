## Design notes

- Short of money for a drink, he asks whoever he meets (the topic is `money` about himself, which marks trouble, so the kindness rule can bring gifts).
- At most two asks a day. If nothing came of an ask by his next look, he will not ask that person again this week.
- Tilly keeps away from him.
