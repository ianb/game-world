# Greta

Lives in the hamlet, the smithy; keeps the store. Program: `crates/characters/src/greta.rs`.

## Description

Bram's wife. Keeps the hamlet store.

Placeholder, written to get the valley running. Rename and rewrite at will.

## Design notes

- Sells candles to anyone in the shop while she keeps it; short of money, it goes on the slate (`owed`).
- Meeting a debtor anywhere, she asks for what is owed (a topic marked as money trouble) and takes it if they have it.
- Fetches store goods from the station shed on train days, up to what the shelf should hold. Tea, sugar and tobacco she sells at what the railway charges her. Orders candles from outside on Monday mornings only when the shelf is low.
- Customers: Amos (Mon), Rosa (Tue), Ottilie (Wed), Iris (Thu).
