# Lottie

Lives in the old town, the Pike house. Program: `worlds/valley/characters/src/lottie.rs`.

## Description

Hester's granddaughter, seventeen. Wants to leave the valley.

Placeholder, written to get the valley running. Rename and rewrite at will.

## Design notes

- On Wednesday and Sunday afternoons she walks to `road-out` and looks down the road for forty minutes, thinking about leaving.
- Jory is sweet on her and calls on her on Sundays.
- On days the train stops she is at the platform ten minutes before it comes and stays until it has gone, then thinks about leaving; a quarter of the time she says so aloud (`lottie::SAYS_SO`).
