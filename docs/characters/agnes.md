# Agnes

Lives in the west end, the school (a room behind the schoolroom). Program: `worlds/valley/characters/src/agnes.rs`.

## Description

The schoolmistress. Strict and exact, a perfectionist, not warm but fair. Teaches Kit, Tilly and Benny.

Placeholder, written to get the valley running. Rename and rewrite at will.

## Design notes

- At half past nine on school days she notes which pupils are not at their desks (`late`).
- Out of school, when she meets a parent of a pupil late twice or more, she tells them (`being-late`) and clears the count.
- Kit is late on some mornings because he goes looking for Pip first, so the loop runs.
- Accepts Celia's tea invitations: if Celia spoke of tea within her hearing in the last four days, she goes to the manor on Thursday.
